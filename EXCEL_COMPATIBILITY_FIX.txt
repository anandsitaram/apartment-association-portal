Excel compatibility fix

The public/template.xlsx workbook has been normalized through LibreOffice to remove legacy/unsupported style extension metadata that was being carried into generated exports. This keeps the template content while removing unused slicer/timeline style extension lists that can trigger strict spreadsheet-reader compatibility issues.

The user-provided generated workbooks were also opened and re-saved through LibreOffice as Excel-compatible copies:
- RV_Fallon_Sept_2026_ExcelCompatible.xlsx
- RV_Fallon_Sept_2026_Complete_ExcelCompatible.xlsx
